📊 BOQ — Bills of Quantities

A Bill of Quantities is the foundation of every successful construction project. It tells you exactly how much material, labor and time your project will need — before you spend a single shilling.

At Gilgal Investments, our BOQ specialists measure every detail of your site, prepare accurate quantities, and deliver a professional document you can take to any contractor, bank or investor.

  • Accurate material take-off
  • Labor and time costing
  • Full project estimation
  • Cost control during construction
  • Prepared by experienced quantity surveyors
  • Accepted by banks, investors & contractors
